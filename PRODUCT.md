# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Practicing software architects and developers who are actively documenting a real system with the arc42 template. Their context is work-in-progress: they have a section open in their editor and come here to answer "what belongs in this section and how do I do it well?" They value their time, know the domain, and want a trusted reference rather than a tutorial. Newcomers to arc42 are a secondary audience and should not feel lost, but the design optimizes for the experienced reader who scans and looks things up.

## Product Purpose

The public documentation site for the arc42 architecture-communication template, published at docs.arc42.org. For each of the 12 arc42 sections it explains the Motivation (why the section is useful), Content (what belongs in it), Form (how to do it, usually with variants), plus practical tips and concrete examples. Success is a reader landing on a section, quickly finding the guidance or example they need, and returning to their own documentation with a clear next step. It is free and community-maintained under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Brand Personality

Expert, clear, pragmatic. The voice is that of an experienced practitioner sharing hard-won advice: confident and direct, never hand-wavy or salesy. It explains without lecturing, offers options without dictating, and trusts the reader's intelligence. Calm authority over hype. The open-source, community-generous spirit of arc42 sits underneath, but the surface tone stays practical and to the point.

## Anti-references

- **Salesy SaaS landing pages.** No gradient hero sections, fabricated metrics, conversion-funnel CTAs, or "sign up now" energy. This is a reference, not a sales page.
- **Heavy enterprise documentation portals.** No dense, cluttered, bureaucratic corporate-docs feel. Avoid walls of chrome, deep nested megamenus, and legalistic tone.
- **Trendy or flashy design.** No neon, glassmorphism, dark-mode-because-it-looks-cool, or gratuitous animation. Nothing that will read as dated in two years.
- **Sterile, generic templates.** No anonymous default-framework look. The site should feel like arc42, not like a Bootstrap starter anyone could have shipped.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** arc42 is about communicating architecture clearly and with structure. The site itself must model that: legible hierarchy, honest structure, nothing decorative that doesn't aid understanding.
2. **Reference-first, not tutorial-first.** Optimize for scanning, jumping, and looking things up. Practitioners arrive mid-task; help them find the answer fast and get back to work.
3. **Content is the hero; design recedes.** Identity comes through restraint and typographic care, not ornament. Every element earns its place by serving the reading.
4. **Expert confidence, no selling.** The content stands on its merit. Present guidance plainly and let quality do the persuading; never lean on marketing gestures.
5. **Structure mirrors arc42.** The disciplined 12-section shape is a feature, not a constraint. Make the section framework and the Motivation/Content/Form rhythm easy to perceive and navigate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target is committed. The bar is simply sensible, readable, and inclusive by default: comfortable contrast and line length for long reading sessions, keyboard-navigable, semantic structure, and respect for reduced-motion preferences. Because the content is text-heavy and read for extended stretches, readability is treated as a first-class concern regardless of a formal conformance level.

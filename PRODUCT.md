# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences of roughly equal weight, both arriving mid-task with a real system to document.

- **Experienced practitioners.** Software architects and developers who already know arc42 and the domain, and come to look something up: "what belongs in this section, and how do I do it well?" They value their time, scan rather than read, and want a trusted reference they can jump into and out of quickly.
- **Newcomers to arc42.** People meeting the template for the first time, or introducing it to a team. They need orientation: what a section is for, why it matters, and a clear first step, without being dragged through a tutorial.

The design serves both. It stays fast to scan and jump around for the expert, and clear to enter and get oriented for the newcomer. Neither audience is an afterthought.

## Product Purpose

The public documentation site for the arc42 architecture-communication template, published at docs.arc42.org. For each of the 12 arc42 sections it explains the Motivation (why the section is useful), Content (what belongs in it), Form (how to do it, usually with variants), plus practical tips and concrete examples. Success is a reader landing on a section, quickly finding the guidance or example they need, and returning to their own documentation with a clear next step. It is one of the arc42 family of sites, alongside arc42.org and quality.arc42.org, and shares their identity. Free and community-maintained under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Brand Personality

Expert, clear, pragmatic, and current. The voice is that of an experienced practitioner sharing hard-won advice: confident and direct, never hand-wavy or salesy. It explains without lecturing, offers options without dictating, and trusts the reader's intelligence. Calm authority over hype. The open-source, community-generous spirit of arc42 sits underneath. The surface should feel modern and fresh, part of the refreshed arc42 family, welcoming and lively rather than austere or dated, while keeping the practical, to-the-point tone.

## Anti-references

- **Salesy marketing pages and brochures.** No gradient hero sections, fabricated metrics, conversion-funnel CTAs, "sign up now" energy, or glossy sales-brochure sheen. This is a reference, not a pitch.
- **Heavy enterprise documentation portals.** No dense, cluttered, bureaucratic corporate-docs feel. Avoid walls of chrome, deep nested megamenus, and legalistic tone.
- **Trendy or gimmicky effects.** Colour and freshness are welcome; neon, glassmorphism, dark-mode-because-it-looks-cool, and gratuitous animation are not. Nothing that will read as dated in two years. Adding colour is not the same as being flashy: colour here carries identity and wayfinding, used with restraint and meaning.
- **Sterile, generic templates.** No anonymous default-framework look. The site should feel like arc42, not like a Bootstrap starter anyone could have shipped.
- **AI-generated slop.** No "an AI clearly made this" tells: identical icon-and-heading card grids, gradient hero-metric templates, decorative glassmorphism, or interchangeable stock layouts.
- **Cluttered information overload.** Nothing that buries the content: no stacked competing callouts, no dense sidebars fighting the text, nothing that puts too much on screen at once. Whitespace and hierarchy over density.
- **Dry academic textbook.** Correct but joyless. No stiff, over-formal, lifeless academic-paper feel. The guidance should read as practical and human.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** arc42 is about communicating architecture clearly and with structure. The site itself must model that: legible hierarchy, honest structure, nothing decorative that does not aid understanding.
2. **Fast to scan, easy to enter.** Optimize for two moves at once: the expert scanning, jumping, and looking things up, and the newcomer getting oriented and finding a first step. Both should reach the answer fast.
3. **Content is the hero; design gives it clarity and energy.** Identity comes through typographic care and purposeful colour, not ornament. Colour and freshness earn their place when they aid reading, wayfinding, or arc42 identity; every element still serves the content.
4. **Expert confidence, no selling.** The content stands on its merit. Present guidance plainly and let quality persuade; never lean on marketing gestures.
5. **Structure mirrors arc42.** The disciplined 12-section shape is a feature, not a constraint. Make the section framework and the Motivation/Content/Form rhythm easy to perceive and navigate.
6. **One arc42 family.** Feel like a sibling of arc42.org and quality.arc42.org: modern, fresh, and colour-aware, sharing their visual language so the family reads as one. Never dated, never off on its own.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target is committed, though the wider arc42 family now tunes for AA and this site should follow that spirit. The bar is sensible, readable, and inclusive by default: comfortable contrast and line length for long reading sessions, keyboard-navigable, semantic structure, and respect for reduced-motion preferences. Because the content is text-heavy and read for extended stretches, readability is a first-class concern. As colour becomes more prominent, two rules follow: keep every colour pairing comfortably legible, and never let colour be the sole carrier of meaning, always pairing it with text, icon, or shape.

A small facet for pages where the form *is* the content: the tip does not merely mention a table,
its columns are what it asks you to think about ([[tip-4-2]]: quality goal, scenario, solution
approach, link to details).

Related but distinct from [[notation]], which covers diagram types and the tools that draw them.
A table needs no notation and no tool, which is why arc42 reaches for one so often.

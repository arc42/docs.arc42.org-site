For readers facing something too big to draw on one page. The answer arc42 keeps giving is the
same in two sections: open one element into a diagram of its own and leave the rest alone.
[[tip-7-4]] applies it to the [[deployment-view]] — "especially useful in heterogeneous or
distributed systems" — and points at the building block view for the pattern it copies.

A facet rather than a concept: the levels themselves belong to whatever is being decomposed.

For readers who have two structures and need to say which part of one lives in which part of the
other. In section 7 that is the [[building-block|building blocks]] — strictly, the artifacts built
from them — onto the [[hardware]], drawn as a diagram ([[tip-7-6]]) or written as a table
([[tip-7-7]]).

A facet: what is mapped onto what differs per section, so the pages under it are meant to be read
for the technique.

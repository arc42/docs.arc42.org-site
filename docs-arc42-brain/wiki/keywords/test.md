For readers who would rather read an executable statement than a paragraph. [[tip-8-8]] is the
argument: a unit test states its prerequisites in the setup and its consequences in the
assertions, so it documents a concept without the documentation being able to drift from it.

A facet next to [[source-code]], which marks pages about the relationship to the implementation in
general; this one marks the narrower case where the *tests* are the artefact being pointed at.

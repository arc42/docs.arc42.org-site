**Definition.** The business or project objective the system is built to serve, stated at a higher
level than any individual system requirement.

**In arc42.** Section 1's introduction counts the "underlying business goals" among the driving
forces an architect must know, and [[tip-1-1]] asks for them to be expressed clearly and
concisely at the top of section 1.1. [[tip-1-3]] adds that they should already exist — in the
project contract or the requirements specification — and that the architect's job is to make sure
they are explicitly known rather than to invent them.

**Distinguish from.** [[quality-goal]] — section 1.2 warns explicitly: "We really mean quality
goals for the architecture. Don't confuse them with project goals. They are not necessarily
identical." A business goal is a reason for building the system; a quality goal is a property the
architecture must have.

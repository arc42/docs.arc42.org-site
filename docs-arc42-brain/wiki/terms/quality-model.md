**Definition.** A published catalogue of quality characteristics, used to find the right term for
a quality and to check that none was forgotten.

**In arc42.** Section 1.2 shows the nine characteristics of ISO/IEC 25010:2023 as an overview of
potential topics, and [[tip-1-14]] uses the same standard as a checklist of top-level quality
topics. arc42 also publishes its own, the arc42 quality model Q42 at
<https://quality.arc42.org>: around 150 example requirements and some 190 quality characteristics
([[tip-1-24]]). [[tip-1-15]] explains what the examples are for — asking stakeholders to name
their quality requirements produces silence or buzzwords, while reacting to a concrete example is
easy, so the catalogue supplies the example to argue with.

**Distinguish from.** [[quality-tree]] — a quality model is a general catalogue that exists
independently of any system; a quality tree is one system's own hierarchy, built in section 10,
often by picking from a quality model.

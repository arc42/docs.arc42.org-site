**Definition.** One of the top three to five quality requirements for the architecture, the ones
whose fulfilment matters most to the major stakeholders.

**In arc42.** Section 1.2 holds only this handful, as a table of goals with concrete scenarios
ordered by priority; everything else belongs in section 10 ([[tip-1-16]], [[tip-1-18]]).
[[tip-1-11]] warns that quality goals are the requirements most likely to stay implicit, and
[[tip-1-13]] says that an explicit assumption about them — an educated guess, written as a
scenario and discussed with stakeholders — beats having none. Where a quality goal drove a
decision, [[tip-1-17]] puts goal and decision together in a table in section 4 and leaves only a
reference here.

**Distinguish from.** [[quality-requirement]] — the same kind of statement; "quality goal" is the
role it plays when it is one of the few most important ones and appears in section 1.2.
[[business-goal]] — a project objective, which section 1.2 warns is not the same thing.

**Definition.** A hierarchical breakdown of one system's quality requirements, from broad quality
topics down to the individual scenarios that make them measurable.

**In arc42.** It lives in section 10, which is where [[tip-1-16]] and [[tip-1-18]] send every
quality requirement that is not one of section 1.2's top three to five. [[tip-1-14]] uses the
hierarchical shape of ISO 25010 as the checklist for the upper levels.

**Distinguish from.** [[quality-model]] — the model is the published catalogue to pick from; the
tree is this system's selection, with its own priorities and scenarios.

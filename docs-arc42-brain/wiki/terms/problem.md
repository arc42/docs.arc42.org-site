**Definition.** A defect or weakness the system has now, whose cost is already being paid.

**In arc42.** Section 11's tips ask for problems and risks in one breath — all six say "problems
and risks" — because the places you look are the same: the stakeholders who know where it hurts,
the interfaces, an evaluation against the requirements, the processes, the data, the code. The
distinction matters for what you do next, not for how you find them, which is why arc42 keeps the
pair together rather than splitting section 11 in two.

**Distinguish from.** [[risk]] — a risk can still be avoided and may cost nothing; a problem is
already costing something. [[technical-debt]] — debt is a problem the team took on knowingly and
can name the reason for.

**Definition.** Any person, role or organization that should know the architecture, has to be
convinced of it, has to work with it or with the code, needs its documentation for their work, or
has to come up with decisions about the system or its development.

**In arc42.** Section 1.3 asks for an explicit overview of them, as a table of role name, person
name and expectations; they determine the extent and the level of detail of the documentation.
In section 9 they appear twice: they should be able to comprehend and retrace the decisions
(the section's motivation), and they are the people to ask for the decision criteria (tip 9-2).

**Distinguish from.** [[quality-requirement]] — stakeholders *have* expectations; a quality
requirement is such an expectation made specific and measurable.

**Definition.** A notation for business processes, explicitly aimed at business readers rather
than at developers.

**In arc42.** Section 6's *Form* lists "BPMN or EPCs (event process chains)" among the notations
for runtime scenarios. [[tip-1-7]] offers it in section 1 for the same reason it exists: when
stakeholders perceive an [[activity-diagram]] as too technical, BPMN describes the same business
or technical process in a form they will read.

**Distinguish from.** [[activity-diagram]] — the same job in UML, for a more technical audience.
The choice between them is about who has to read the diagram, not about what it can express.

**Note on the name.** [[tip-1-7]] spells it out as "Business Process Model Notation"; the
standard's own name is Business Process Model **and** Notation. Both spellings are aliases here so
that either resolves.

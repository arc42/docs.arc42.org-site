Navigate by keyword

Three ways in, depending on how much rigour your project needs.

  • lean Shorten and streamline your documentation pragmatically; travel light.
  • thorough Formal, large, or critical systems that demand accuracy and auditability.
  • essential The things you should always document, whatever your process.

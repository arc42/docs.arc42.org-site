Three directions for the refreshed site

Same content, three distinct takes on layout, colour, and typography. Open each proposal's home page and its content page (section 3, Context and Scope) to compare like for like.

Each proposal answers the same brief drawn from PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, and the /impeccable critique: a calm reading surface, colour concentrated in the chrome and callouts, a correct heading outline, comfortable measure, real focus states and wayfinding, and the arc42 family's Signal Blue. They diverge on personality and, in proposal C, on the navigation model itself.

Proposal A

Field Guide, refreshed

Classic left sidebar · on-spec

The safe, correct refresh: a confident deep-blue masthead, the familiar left sidebar polished and fixed, an editorial-serif heading voice. Aligned to the arc42 family type target.

  • Fonts · Libre Caslon Text + Atkinson Hyperlegible
  • Colour · DESIGN.md palette, deep-blue band
  • Nav · left rail, fixed with active state

Proposal B

Engineer's Handbook

Three-pane docs · on-page TOC

A precise software-manual take: a three-pane layout with a right-hand on-page table of contents, flatter surfaces, cool slate neutrals, one committed workhorse sans with strong weight contrast.

  • Fonts · Public Sans + Spline Sans Mono
  • Colour · cool slate, slim navy masthead
  • Nav · left rail + right on-page TOC

Proposal C · alternative nav

The Spine

12-section numbered spine · stepper

Rethinks the sidebar: a slim vertical spine of the numbers 1–12 that mirrors arc42's structure, names revealing on hover/focus, plus a prev/next stepper. The freshest, liveliest face.

  • Fonts · Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk
  • Colour · warmer, coral + amber accents
  • Nav · numbered spine (the alternative)

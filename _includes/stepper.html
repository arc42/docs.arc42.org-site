{%- comment -%} Previous / next pager at the foot of a template section page. Driven entirely by _data/sections.yml, joined on the page's front-matter `number:`. It replaces the training advert that used to close a section page. The advert stays on _layouts/page.html and _layouts/post.html — this only displaces it where the reader has an obvious next move. Three corrections to the mockup's version: · its 1px --hairline borders measure 1.26:1 on paper, i.e. invisible. These are --hairline-2, the token that exists for borders that must be seen. · prev and next were styled identically, so the design recommended nothing at the one moment it is there to recommend something. "Next" carries the --signal border and the --row-active fill; "previous" is quiet. · each box now carries the target section's one-line blurb, so "4. Solution Strategy" tells the reader what they would be going to. Layout is a two-column grid rather than flex, so section 1 (no previous) and section 12 (no next) keep their single box in the correct half. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign here = page.number | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign prev_nr = here | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign next_nr = here | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign prev = site.data.sections | where: 'number', prev_nr | first -%} {%- assign next = site.data.sections | where: 'number', next_nr | first -%} {%- if prev or next -%}

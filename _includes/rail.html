{%- comment -%} The left navigation rail. The twelve sections come from _data/sections.yml — the single source of truth — not from a `site.pages | sort: 'order'` loop, which used to mix template sections and meta pages into one undifferentiated list. The "More" group is every page carrying `group: meta` in its front matter (currently Examples, Keywords, Contact), sorted by `order`. A page opts into the rail by declaring the group; nothing here needs editing to add one. `nav_title` wins over `title` when present, so a page can carry a long editorial title ("All tips (by keyword)") and a short rail label. Home is intentionally absent from the list: the masthead logo and wordmark are the link home on every page. The rail is NOT rendered on /home/ — see _layouts/default.html. Mobile: the markup for the toggle is here, the behaviour is in assets/js/site.js. The contract is exactly: button.rail-toggle[aria-expanded] toggles #rail-body.open {%- endcomment -%}

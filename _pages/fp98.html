THE PALETTE IS THE WINDOWS 16-COLOUR VGA SET, and that constraint is the whole design. #c0c0c0 silver, #008080 teal, #000080 navy, #ffff00 yellow, #ff0000 red, #800080 purple, black, white. No arc42 brand colour appears anywhere: the deep blue and the coral spark are *pretty*, and this page is under instruction not to be. Nothing here reads _tokens.scss and nothing here should. Every fact below is generated from _data/sections.yml, the same source the real home page, the rail, the stepper and the breadcrumb read. Nothing is hand-copied, so the 1998 page cannot drift away from the 2026 one. The standing no-counts rule (never WRITE how many examples, sections or tips exist) is honoured: the tip counts are computed at build time exactly as _pages/home.md computes them. No number on this page is typed. The clip art is hand-drawn pixel SVG, inline. It is deliberately NOT lifted from a real FrontPage theme or a GIF archive: period Microsoft theme art is still under copyright, and a 16x16 grid of costs less than sourcing a licence. {%- endcomment -%}

ABOUT THIS PAGE

You have reached fp98 — the FrontPage 98 edition of docs.arc42.org. It is a period reconstruction: what this documentation site might have looked like had it been published in 1998 and built in Microsoft FrontPage 98.

The content is not a mock-up. The twelve sections, their descriptions and their tip counts come from the same source of truth the modern site reads, so this page cannot fall out of date. Only the presentation is from 1998, and the links lead to the real, current pages — which is where the illusion ends.
